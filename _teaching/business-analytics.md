An applied course designed to teach decision-making driven by business quantitative tools, financial data modeling, and executive visual analytics.

### Key Topics Covered:
* Predictive modeling and forecasting techniques for business decisions
* Building interactive dashboards and dynamic reporting using Power BI
* Key Performance Indicator (KPI) tracking and variance analysis
* Data-driven decision support systems for corporate and market strategy
