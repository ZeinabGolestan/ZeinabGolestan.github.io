A foundational course providing essential statistical concepts required for quantitative economic modeling and empirical research.

### Key Topics Covered:
* Probability theory, random variables, and theoretical distributions
* Sampling techniques and Estimation Theory
* Hypothesis testing and Confidence Intervals
* Correlation analysis and intro to statistical inference
