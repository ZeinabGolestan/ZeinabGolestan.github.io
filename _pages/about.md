Welcome to my personal academic website! 

I am an economist, data analyst, and university lecturer with expertise in econometrics, statistical analysis, and data-driven decision-making.

My work focuses on helping organizations, researchers, and development projects transform complex data into actionable insights. By combining academic rigor with practical analytics, I support evidence-based decision-making, performance improvement, and impactful research outcomes.

I have experience in quantitative research, econometric modeling, survey design and analysis, impact evaluation, and statistical consulting. My expertise extends to business intelligence, KPI development, data visualization, and dashboard design, enabling organizations to better understand their data and make informed strategic decisions.

My areas of expertise include:

📊 Econometric Modeling & Statistical Analysis
📈 Data Analytics & Business Intelligence
📋 Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E)
🎯 Impact Evaluation
📑 Research Design & Survey Analysis
📉 Data Visualization & Tableau Dashboards
🔍 Research & Data Analytics Consulting

Beyond consulting, I am passionate about teaching and capacity building. I deliver training and workshops in econometrics, applied statistics, research methodology, Stata, Tableau, and data analytics, helping researchers and professionals strengthen their analytical skills and make better use of data.

I am particularly interested in collaborating with universities, NGOs, international development organizations, research institutions, and private sector partners on research, monitoring & evaluation, data analytics, and evidence-based policy initiatives.

Let’s connect if you’re looking for support in analytics, evaluation, research, or data-driven decision-making.
