### Abstract
This study investigates the causal nexus of imports, exports, and economic growth in Uzbekistan using time series data (1995–2023) and advanced econometric techniques. Employing the Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) unit root test, Johansen cointegration test, and a Vector Error Correction Model (VECM), the findings reveal unidirectional causality from imports to exports, alongside bidirectional causal relationships between imports and economic growth, as well as between exports and economic growth. The results highlight the necessity of a balanced trade policy fostering both import growth and export promotion to sustain long-term economic development in Uzbekistan.

### Key Highlights
* Applied Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) tests confirming $I(1)$ integration and Johansen cointegration confirming a long-run equilibrium relationship.
* Utilized Vector Error Correction Model (VECM) and Wald causality tests to evaluate short-run and long-run dynamic adjustments.
* Demonstrated that imported capital and intermediate inputs directly drive export performance in Uzbekistan.
