### Abstract
This study examines the dynamic causal relationships between Foreign Direct Investment (FDI), economic growth, and trade openness in Uzbekistan over the period 1997–2023. Employing Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) unit root tests, Johansen cointegration tests, and a Vector Error Correction Model (VECM), the empirical results confirm long-run equilibrium relationships and bidirectional causality across short-run and long-run horizons among all three variables. The findings underscore the critical role of coordinated trade liberalization and investment policy frameworks in promoting sustainable macroeconomic growth in Uzbekistan.

### Key Highlights
* Empirical application of ADF unit root tests establishing $I(1)$ stationarity and Johansen cointegration confirming long-run equilibrium vectors.
* Deployment of Vector Error Correction Model (VECM) to confirm robust bidirectional causality among FDI, trade openness, and GDP.
* Policy implications highlighting the synergistic feedback mechanisms between trade liberalization, foreign investment attraction, and long-term economic growth.
