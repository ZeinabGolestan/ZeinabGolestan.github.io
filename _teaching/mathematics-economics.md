This course covers core mathematical principles and analytical tools indispensable for rigorous economic theory, optimization, and modeling.

### Key Topics Covered:
* Differential calculus and multivariable optimization in micro/macroeconomics
* Linear algebra: Matrix operations, determinants, and systems of linear equations
* Constrained optimization (Lagrange multipliers) and economic applications
* Integral calculus and dynamic economic modeling fundamentals
