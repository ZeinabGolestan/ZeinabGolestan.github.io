### Abstract
This paper investigates the infrastructure, organizational capacity, and analytical framework required for adopting Big Data technologies and data-driven decision-making within the school education system of Uzbekistan.

### Key Highlights
* Empirical and qualitative assessment of digital readiness in Uzbekistan educational institutions.
* Strategic framework for integrating Big Data tools to improve institutional decision-making and educational policy.
