### Abstract
This study examines income convergence and evaluates the impact of bilateral trade and institutional quality on income convergence in Central Asia across the period from 2003 to 2023. Employing beta and sigma convergence tests alongside panel econometric techniques (Fixed Effects and GMM), the empirical findings confirm income convergence in Central Asia. Bilateral trade and capital stock exhibit a positive and significant effect on income convergence, while institutional quality and labor force growth show negative effects.

### Key Highlights
* Empirical validation of beta ($\beta$) and sigma ($\sigma$) income convergence in Central Asian economies (2003–2023).
* Quantitative evaluation of bilateral trade networks and physical capital accumulation in fostering regional integration.
* Robust estimation using Fixed Effects (FE) and Generalized Method of Moments (GMM) estimators.
