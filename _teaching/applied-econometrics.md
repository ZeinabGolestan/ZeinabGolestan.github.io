Hands-on practical course focusing on advanced empirical estimation techniques and interactive business intelligence tools for economic analysis.

### Key Topics Covered:
* Stochastic Frontier Analysis (SFA) and efficiency estimation
* Advanced panel data modeling using Stata
* Constructing data tables and interactive visual dashboards in Power BI Desktop
* Empirical research design for agricultural and macroeconomic policy evaluation
